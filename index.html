Your store is leaking money.

Margin Leak Scan reads your own Shopify orders and product cost exports and shows you exactly which discounts and products are eating your margin — with every euro traced back to the data it came from.

  • Runs entirely in your browser — your files are never uploaded anywhere
  • Uses your standard Shopify CSV exports — no app install, no login
  • Every figure shows exactly how it was calculated

What this scan checks

Discount leakage

Which discount codes and products absorb disproportionate discount — with real evidence, not just "big numbers".

Low & negative product margin

Sales priced below product cost after discount, and thin-margin products at risky volume — when you provide product costs.

This prototype does not yet analyze returns, shipping cost, payment fees or ad spend — see "What's not included" on every result.
